Medifyles · brand mark v1

The M-folder

A document pocket whose top edge is cut into an M, holding a saffron paper safely inside. It says what the product does (your papers, kept safe and in order) and carries the initial, without a cross, a heartbeat or a shield.

Three directions

Explored side by side at the same size. The recommended one is outlined.


Recommended

1 · M-folder

Unique silhouette (pocket + M + paper). Reads at 16px as a navy tile with a notch. Ownable and trademark-friendly.

2 · Ordered stack

Three sheets squared up: tells the "mess to order" story. But stacked cards are a common icon for layers and storage apps, so it's weaker as a trademark.

3 · Paper + pulse

A document with a heartbeat line. Instantly "health", but the most generic of the three: dozens of clinic apps use it. Shown to rule it out.

Rules

  • Clear space: keep empty space around the mark equal to the width of one shoulder of the M (about ⅕ of the mark).
  • Minimum size: 16px on screen with the favicon version; 24px for the full mark; 10mm in print.
  • Colours: Navy #1E3A75 and Saffron #F5B862. On dark backgrounds use the white-pocket version.
  • Don't rotate, outline, add shadows or gradients, change the colours, or put the mark on busy photos.
